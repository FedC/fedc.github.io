About

Federico Commisso — software engineer behind OKCoded

OKCoded is an independent software engineering and digital solutions practice. One founder, end-to-end delivery, and a focus on products that solve real business problems.

I design and build digital products across the stack: frontend architecture, backend systems, integrations, and the operational details that keep software useful after launch.

Clients come for websites, web applications, mobile work, custom software, and automation. Hiring managers reviewing this site will find the same thread — technical ownership, product judgment, and clear communication.

OKCoded is not a large agency and does not pretend to be one. Engagements stay scoped, honest about constraints, and oriented toward shipping.

Process

Discovery, build, launch, support

  1. Discovery

    Clarify audience, constraints, and what “done” means before writing code.

  2. Build

    Ship structured product surfaces, systems, or integrations with performance and accessibility in mind.

  3. Launch

    Deploy cleanly, verify tracking where it matters, and confirm behavior on real devices.

  4. Ongoing support

    Optional follow-through for updates, improvements, and keeping things healthy after release.
