About

An independent studio for practical web work

OKCoded helps businesses establish, improve, and grow their online presence — without the noise of a oversized agency pitch.

OKCoded is the studio practice of Federico Commisso: frontend engineering, interaction design, and the digital operations that keep a site useful after launch.

Work spans marketing sites, product interfaces, SEO foundations, analytics setup, light automation, and ongoing maintenance. The goal is clear delivery — not a stack of buzzwords.

Process

Discovery, build, launch, support

  1. Discovery

    Clarify audience, constraints, and what “done” means before writing code.

  2. Build

    Ship structured pages, product UI, or integrations with performance and accessibility in mind.

  3. Launch

    Deploy cleanly, verify tracking, and make sure the site behaves on real devices.

  4. Ongoing support

    Optional maintenance for updates, small improvements, and keeping things online.
